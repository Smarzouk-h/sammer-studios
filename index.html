About

A photographer
inside medicine

Five years behind a camera, and an MD/PhD student at Northwestern.

That combination is the reason I am comfortable in the rooms other photographers find awkward: hospitals, labs, lecture halls, a building on its opening day. It is also why I know exactly what an ERAS headshot has to do, because I am on the same road as the people sitting for them.

The rest is engagements, graduations, events, and product work for small businesses. Then there is the Arabic calligraphy, in ink, which started separately and quietly became part of the job.
