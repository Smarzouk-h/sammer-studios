Good to know

The small print

Cropping. Most of these photographs are 3:2. An 8×10 or a 16×20 is 5:4, so a little of the long edge has to go. Say the word in your order notes and I will send you a proof of the crop before anything is printed.

Colour. Screens are brighter than paper. Prints come out slightly deeper than what you see here, which is usually what you want on a wall.

Payment. Nothing is taken when you submit an order. Once I confirm it, you pay by Venmo or PayPal and I send the job to print. The buttons appear as soon as your order goes through.

Something bigger? If you want a size that is not listed, or a print of something you saw on my Instagram that is not on this site, just ask.
