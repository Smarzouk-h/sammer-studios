Questions

Asked often

How far will you travel?
Chicago is covered by the listed price. Beyond the city I charge travel at cost and tell you the number before you commit.

When do I get the photos?
A preview set within 48 hours, the full edited gallery within a week. Faster if you have a deadline, say so.

What if it rains?
We reschedule, no charge. Overcast light is usually better anyway; genuine downpours are not.

Can I print the photos myself?
Yes. Personal-use print release comes with every session. If you would rather I handled printing, the print shop does that too.

Do you do student rates?
Yes. Mention it when you write. I am a student too.

I hate being photographed.
Most people say this. It is mostly a matter of not being rushed, and of me not making you stare down a lens for an hour.
